Privacy Policy — Olivia Babil

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Olivia Babil (“we”, “us”) — a small retail business that prices and orders SHEIN shopping carts for customers in Iraq — handles information when you contact us on Instagram and our automated pricing assistant replies to you.

1. What information we process

  • Your Instagram message — specifically the SHEIN shopping-cart link you send us, so we can price it for you.
  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID — the identifier Instagram provides for our conversation, used only to send our reply back to you.
  • The computed price and item count for the cart you shared.

We do not collect your name, phone number, address, payment details, or any other personal data through this automated Instagram assistant.

2. How we use it

We use your message solely to: (a) read the SHEIN cart link you sent, (b) calculate the total price and number of items, and (c) reply to you on Instagram with that price. That is the only purpose.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not store your messages longer than needed to reply to you.

4. Data retention & deletion

Message content is processed in real time to generate a price reply and is not retained in a long-term database. You may request deletion of any information associated with you at any time by sending us a direct message on Instagram (@olivia.babil) or by email (see below), and we will remove it promptly.

5. Third-party services

Our assistant operates through Meta’s Instagram Platform (the Instagram Messaging API). Your use of Instagram is also governed by Meta’s own Privacy Policy and Terms. We access Instagram data only with the permissions you and the business account have authorized.

6. Children

Our service is intended for adults shopping for retail products and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Contact

For any privacy question or a data-deletion request, contact us:
